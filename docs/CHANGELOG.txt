CHANGELOG
=========
0.3.1 (hotfix, Nov 22 2026) - plugin v1.48
- NEW: the Extended Menu works with keyboard and mouse only (no controller needed). F2 on the pause screen opens it
  (or Esc -> Options -> Extended Menu); arrows / W A S D move and change, Enter / Space open sections, Q / E tabs,
  Tab sections, R default, Esc / Backspace / F2 save and close. Before, it closed at once without a controller.
  README section 4a has the full key list. Key: [Enhancements] KeyboardKey=F2 (NONE = off).
- The pause screen shows "F2  Extended Menu" when no controller is connected; the menu's button line shows keys
  after keyboard use. Controllers work exactly as before.

0.3.0 (pre-release, Nov 21 2026) - plugin v1.47, Costume Launcher 1.0
COSTUMES
- NEW: Costume tab in the Extended menu (the last tab, after Cheats & Debug). Pick any costume from your
  library and the Punisher changes AT ONCE, even mid-level. 'Reload costume files' picks up new ones while the game
  runs; optional keyboard key [Costume] NextKey (e.g. F7). Costumes no longer go into the level memory, so big HD
  textures no longer hang the loading screen.
- NEW: outfit switch - Level's own / Trench coat / T-shirt in every level (from the next level or checkpoint). New
  installer part 'outfits'. In the T-shirt the pistols sit in the holsters and long guns go on the back, like in the
  T-shirt levels.
- NEW: the PC version's exclusive T-shirt skins as costumes: PC Exclusive 1980 / 1990 / 2000.
- NEW: in-game skulls follow the costume (HUD, enemy and hotspot icons, menus); the worn skull is used only above
  enemies and on interrogation / kill hotspots. Costume tab -> In-game skulls.
- NEW: cutscenes show your costume's textures on the trench-coat Punisher.
- Black Colt: now on the Costume tab -> Weapon Skin -> Colt .45 (Original / 04 Movie). True black now (the 0.2 one
  had a green tint that showed as green spots; the installer fixes an installed one), and it stays black in the
  holsters.
KILLS AND GORE
- FIX: meat hook kill - fat thugs use their own animation and now hang right on the hook (no rack clipping).
- NEW: the tiki torch kill (Central Zoo) gets the meat hook kill's blood: wound marks, spray, spurts, the drip from the
  wound and the floor pool. Same switch: Gore -> Special Interrogations & Kills. The installer part 'hooks' adds the
  drip / pool effects to your zoo1.vpp.
- FIX: meat hook / tiki pools stay on the floor (they could float when you stood higher), the drip keeps going.
MENU AND CONTROLS
- Pause + Y opens the dark Extended menu; in-game menu -> Options -> Extended Menu now shows the paper look like the
  main menu (the installer part 'menuart' adds the paper to mini.vpp).
- Cheats & Debug tab shown by default, before Costume.
- Keyboard: keys no longer reach the game while the Extended menu is open; Esc / Backspace close it. Keys set in the
  ini show their name (e.g. "Left Ctrl (from the ini)").
- Photo mode: keyboard key [Photo] Key=F9; pause and the in-game menu work in photo mode (the in-game menu could
  freeze before).
SETTINGS
- Black & white: kill effects default Off, Slaughter mode On. Our settings win over other mods (e.g. ermaccer's
  PunisherPlugin); 'Game / other mods' lets the other mod decide.
COSTUME LAUNCHER (first public release, 1.0)
- Kit builder (parts, skulls, shirt backgrounds), costume library, smooth blacks, Default HD source; buttons 'Send
  changes to the game' (only what changed), 'Send whole library to the game', 'Send to in-game picker'.
- Texture templates are a separate download on Nexus.

0.2.0 (pre-release, Nov 20) - plugin v1.09
- NEW: the meat-hook quick kill in the Meat Packing Plant (restored, on all 23 hooks), with our own blood: wall
  marks, spray from the chest wound, drip and pool. New installer part "hooks".
- NEW: one switch for everything we add to the special interrogations and quick kills: Extended menu -> Gore ->
  "Special Interrogations & Kills" (Extended / Original): meat hook, semi trailer, casket, propeller and fridge
  blood. Every kill we remaster from now on goes under it.
- NEW: redesigned Extended menu - pages in a dark sidebar with the faded skull and a torn edge, settings over the
  dimmed paused game, the game's white brush bar on the selected row, help in its own box. Uses the game's own menu
  art (new installer part "menuart"); [Enhancements] Layout=0 = the old text layout.
- FIX (GitHub #1): the Extended menu opened from Options inside a level could flicker - it stretched a picture the
  level does not load behind itself. Inside a level it now uses the new layout.
- CHANGED: X in the Extended menu resets only the highlighted setting (it used to reset every setting at once).
- CHANGED: in the Extended menu LT goes up a page and RT down (also with dual pistols in hand); the gun tension
  camera moved to Restored content and its 2004 settings are called "Beta"; long names and values shrink to fit.
- NEW: black Colt for the Punisher only. Your own Colt .45 (one or two) and its HUD / pickup picture in black with
  a silver compensator; enemies keep the normal Colt. Extended menu -> Weapons -> Black Colt (on / off, works at
  once). New installer part "blackcolt": made from your own Colt texture and added to mini.vpp.
- NEW: photo mode hides the crosshair; the grenade / throwable counter uses the digital font too (Digital ammo
  counter); the digital counters are drawn 15% bigger with a thin dark edge ([Beta] DigitalAmmoScale), and the edge
  fades out with the numbers on a weapon switch.

0.1.8 (tester build, Nov 17) - plugin v0.82
- FIX: on 1080p and bigger screens (HUD size Auto or any size above 100 %) the captions, subtitles and some bar
  fills were cut off - the text grew with the HUD but the box it is drawn in did not. Now both grow together.
  If anything on the HUD still looks cut or too big: Extended menu -> HUD & Display -> HUD size (try 150 % or
  Off = the game's size) and tell us your screen resolution.

0.1.7 (tester build, Nov 17) - plugin v0.81
- NEW: Extended menu -> HUD & Display -> "Shadow distance (m)". The game only drew the real shadow within 5 m of
  the camera (a wide FOV counts as further away), so with a big FOV or a far camera Frank's shadow went faint.
  Default now 12 m (game 5, up to 40). Works right away. Raise it further for a very wide FOV; lower it if a busy
  room gets slow (enemies in range get real shadows too).
- Everything from 0.1.6 (level memory 128 MB, blowtorch hand fit, kill spot log).

0.1.6 (tester build, Nov 17) - plugin v0.80
- Level memory is now 128 MB by default (the game had 32.5 MB per level): room for HD / smooth-black costume
  textures - every heavy level tested on the Steam Deck. An existing ExtendedCut.ini keeps its own value: set
  Extended menu -> Settings -> Level memory MB to 128 yourself, then restart the game.
- Developer mode -> Log my position also lists the kill spots near you (for placing new hotspots).
- Blowtorch interrogation: the torch now sits in the Punisher's hand. The spot was set by eye before (4.5 cm off);
  now calculated from the game's own hold animations (Deck-tested). Running the installer again moves it on an
  existing install.

0.1.5 (tester build, Nov 16) - plugin v0.79
- NEW: restored gib stumps. The game's stump models were turned INWARD before release (only a flat cone shows);
  turned back out they show torn meat and bone (the neck matches the 2004 store-kiosk attract video).
  Gore -> "Gib stumps": Original (the cones) / Restored (the developers' stumps as found) / Random variation (a
  different one each gib: restored, bloodier, slim legs, cone legs, tucked arms). Gore presets set it too.
  New installer part "stumps": the stump files are BUILT from your own mini.vpp and added next to the originals.
- FIX: Profiles screen - the stick and D-pad left / right now switch between New / Delete and the profile names.

0.1.1 (tester build, Nov 14)
- FIX: the "pictures" part wrote a broken HUD picture bank into mini.vpp - every HUD icon, the crosshair and the
  menu cursor showed as squares. Fixed; running the installer again repairs an affected mini.vpp by itself.
- The plugin now installs straight into scripts\ (ExtendedCut.asi, ExtendedCut.ini, ExtendedCut.log). Some ASI
  Loader versions do not look into folders inside scripts\, so the plugin did not load. An older install in
  scripts\Extended Cut\ is retired automatically (renamed to .asi.old) and your settings move with it.
- The plugin runs only once even if two copies are found.
- The installer also writes its list to scripts\ExtendedCut_install.log (send it with the plugin logs).
- Fresh install: every button showed "Nothing" (no ExtendedCut.ini yet). The plugin now makes ExtendedCut.ini
  from ExtendedCut_defaults.ini on the first start = the Xbox-style layout.
- Extended Menu: long help texts wrap onto more lines instead of running off the screen.
- Plugin v0.57 -> v0.61 (v0.62 in 0.1.2): "Black & white kill effects" option (also beats other mods that switch it off), fixes for
  bugs in the original game (big fights could run out of room for objects), always-on flashbacks option.
- README: Steam Deck section (setup, launch options, controller, recommended performance settings) and a short
  Costume Launcher guide. install.sh: right-click -> Run In Konsole.
